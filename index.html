About

A designer who builds visual systems, not just pictures.

Yassin Ahmed is a graphic designer specializing in branding, visual identity, social media design, and digital design. The work sits at the intersection of creative thinking and strategic communication — building visual systems that stay consistent across every touchpoint, from a logo mark to a full content calendar.

Every project starts with the same question: what does this brand need to say, and to whom? The answer shapes the typography, the color system, the motion, and the content that follows.
